## Goal

A shopper adds an in-stock product (quantity 1 from a card, or a chosen quantity on the product page), sees the header count change while staying on the page, and manages the cart on `/cart`: change quantity, remove, subtotal from server prices. Quantities above the stock and out-of-stock products are refused with clear messages. The cookie holds ids and quantities only.
