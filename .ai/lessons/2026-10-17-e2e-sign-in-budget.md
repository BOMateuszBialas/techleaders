# 2026-10-17 — The e2e sign-in budget is spent: share the setup's owner session; a generic double-click selector can pay the order

Adding one `signInAsOwner` in a new suite pushed the run over Better Auth's 20 sign-ins per minute, and `admin.e2e.test.ts` (unchanged) failed with "Too many attempts". The global setup now signs in once over HTTP and provides `ownerSessionCookie`; a suite that needs any signed-in owner calls `useOwnerSession(browser)` (`e2e/support/sign-in.ts`) and spends no sign-in. Keep `signInAsOwner` for tests about sign-in itself. Separately, the checkout double-submit test clicked `main form button[type="submit"]` a second time; when the payment page had already loaded, that click confirmed the demo payment, so the test waited for a pay page that had become the confirmation. Scope repeat clicks to the form they mean (`form:has(#checkout-customerName)`).
