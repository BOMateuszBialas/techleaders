## Follow-up, same session (2026-10-14)

- What they described (paraphrase, translated): it is hard even to tell an agency how, and with which tools, analytics should be connected so that it covers 100% of the store's real data. Agencies often suggest one simple tool without server-side analytics. Data coverage is then around 20%, and the agency still recommends changes based on it.
- Verbatim quote (Polish, as supplied): „Często agencja sugeruje jakieś jedno proste narzędzie bez analityki server side i to pokrycie danych jest na 20% i na tej podstawie coś rekomenduje”.
- Interviewer's remarks: "about 20% coverage" is the participant's own figure, not a measurement shared in the session (no store, tool or period given). The account points to an upstream cause: recommendations rest on incomplete client-side tracking data, not only on misinterpretation. Still no single dated case.

## Second follow-up, same session (2026-10-14)

- Asked what the missing ~80% of data consisted of (orders and revenue, or sessions, traffic and conversion), the participant answered: sessions, traffic and conversion (Polish, as supplied: „sesji ruchu i konwersji”).
- Interviewer's remarks: the gap the practitioner describes is behavioural and traffic data, not transactional data. CommerceOS's approved Copilot tools (spec §6) cover orders, line items and stock only, so the MVP does not close this gap.
