# Spacing and layout

One 4 px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96 px (Tailwind `1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24`). Choose spacing by the relation between elements, not by eye: closer means more related. 2, 6 and 10 px are out (the audit found 27 uses); 2 px is allowed only inside a badge.

| Relation                 | Class            | Store                | Admin                          |
| ------------------------ | ---------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Inside a group           | `gap-related`    | 8                    | 8 (4 inside dense table cells) |
| Between items of a group | `gap-group-item` | 16                   | 12                             |
| Between groups           | `gap-group`      | 24                   | 16                             |
| Inside a panel           | `p-panel`        | 24 (product tile 16) | 16                             |
| Between sections         | `py-section`     | 64 (phone 48)        | 32 (phone 24)                  |
| Hero                     | `py-hero`        | 96 (phone 48)        | none                           |
| Page gutter              | `px-gutter`      | 16 / 24 / 32         | 16 / 24                        |

Control heights are component tokens per density: store 40 / 44 / 48 px, admin desktop 28 / 32 / 36 px, and at least 44 px on a phone or a coarse pointer in both (WCAG 2.5.8, rule 16): `h-control-sm`, `h-control-md`, `h-control-lg`.
