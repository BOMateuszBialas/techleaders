# Epic E07: Customer accounts (**P**)

**P:** this epic is outside the stage 0 tree, which ends at E05 (the page is kept for the owner to confirm the scope; P19 on the What is missing page). The backlog has it as E07 (issue-level: customer accounts, D11): registration and sign-in, orders attached to an account, "My orders", data isolation between customers, and the role separation from the owner's admin. The page documents what the app does today.

## Flow

1. A customer signs up (`/account/sign-up`) or signs in (`/account/sign-in`). The demo note says: use any email address; there is no password reset and no email verification.
2. The customer's orders are attached to the account when the customer checks out signed in. A guest checkout is unchanged.
3. "My orders" (`/account/orders`) lists the customer's orders, newest first, up to 50.
4. An order (`/account/orders/[id]`) shows its details. An order that is not the customer's shows the same "Order not found" page as an unknown one.
5. The customer signs out from the header.

## Rules this epic follows

- The customer id comes only from the session, never from the URL or a form. Account reads filter by it in the database (AGENTS.md, customer accounts).
- A foreign or unknown order gives the same "Order not found" page and the same API 404; the page never says "forbidden" (customer accounts spec).
- Owner and customer are separate roles with separate sign-in. An owner session does not open the account area, and a customer session does not open the admin.
- Account dates are shown in English (`en-GB`), in the store's time zone (see Content, P12).
- Checkout follows the mode it showed: a session-read failure never silently places a guest order (AGENTS.md).

## Retrofit (lesson 08)

1. Sign-in and sign-up forms on `Field`, with `FieldError` per field and the demo note as `Alert` `demo`.
2. "My orders" as `Table` with the status as `Badge` text; on a phone, a list of cards (B3.10).
3. `Empty` and `ErrorState` for the states above.
