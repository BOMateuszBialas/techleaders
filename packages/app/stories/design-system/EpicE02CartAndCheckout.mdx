# Epic E02: Cart and checkout

Backlog epic E02 (issue #9): stories E02-S01 (manage a cart), E02-S02 (checkout with server-validated price and stock), E02-S03 (a clearly labelled demo payment and an order confirmation). This is the only flow where red is used for buying, so the rules on the purchase color apply here first.

## Flow

1. The shopper adds a product on the product page. The button shows "Adding…" while the server answers, then "Added to cart." (`role="status"`).
2. The cart (`/cart`) lists the lines. The shopper changes a quantity ("Update") or removes a line ("Remove", with the product name in its accessible name). The cart summary shows the subtotal, "Prices are confirmed at checkout." and the "Checkout" link.
3. If the server changed a line (price, stock), the cart shows "Your cart changed" with the changes and "Update my cart" before checkout continues.
4. Checkout (`/checkout`) asks for the delivery and contact fields. Errors come back from the server and show as one message and per field.
5. "Place order" creates the order with the server's prices and stock. The shopper goes to the demo payment page (`/checkout/pay/[reference]`).
6. The demo payment page carries a standing demo notice. "Confirm demo payment (no charge)" marks the order paid in demo mode.
7. The confirmation page (`/orders/[reference]`) shows "Thank you — demo payment received", the order details and the reference: "Save this reference to check your order."
8. Anyone with the reference can look up an order at `/orders/lookup` ("Find your order", "Find order").

## Rules this epic follows

- The purchase color (`action`) is used for one step in view: "Add to cart" on the product page, "Checkout" in the cart, "Place order" on checkout, "Confirm demo payment (no charge)" on the payment page (tokeny §7 rule 2, batch 1 Q4). It is never on the dark frame (tokeny §7 rule 3).
- The demo notice is `--demo`, not the warning color, and it appears on every page of the payment flow (tokeny rule 11, AGENTS.md principle 6).
- The payment page never looks like a live payment: no card fields, "no charge" in the button, "Paid with: Demo payment (no money charged)".
- The cart's quantity and remove controls keep their names for screen readers: `Remove {product name}`.
- Checkout follows the mode it showed (customer or guest) and never silently places a guest order (customer accounts spec).

## Retrofit (lesson 08)

Spec `.ai/specs/2026-10-26-retrofit-checkout-cart.md`. Phases 1 (cart, drawer, header link, add to cart) and 2 (checkout form and order summary) are built; Phases 3 and 4 are open.

1. Built (Phase 1): the add-to-cart confirmation is an `Alert` `success` with `role="status"`; the cart-changed list is an `Alert` `warning` with `role="status"` (OA-3); the line and drawer messages are `Alert`s (`alert` for errors, `status` for success, B2.18).
2. Built (Phase 2): `Field` around every checkout control, `FieldError` per field; focus moves to the first invalid field after a failed submit (OA-2, `first-invalid-field.ts`); the checkout's loading and unavailable states are `SkeletonGroup` and `ErrorState` (OA-5); the order summary is a `Card`.
3. Built (Phase 1): `action` on Add to cart (product page) and Checkout; Remove is a `link` button (B1.16). Built (Phase 2): Place order is `action` with `aria-busy`. Open (Phase 3): Confirm demo payment.
4. Built (Phase 1): `Empty` for the cart and the drawer; `ErrorState` for the unavailable cart (OA-5). Built (Phase 2): `ErrorState` for the unavailable checkout. Open: the order pages (Phase 3) and the store error page (Phase 4).
5. Open (Phase 3): the demo notice as `Alert` `demo` without a role (B2.18); the payment status as `Badge` with text (P2 mapping).
