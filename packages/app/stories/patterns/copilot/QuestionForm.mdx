# Question form

## What it is for

The place where the owner asks Copilot a question, or picks a suggested one.

## Rules

- The question is a `Field`: its label is bound to the textarea and its hint states the 500-character limit.
- The counter is in the hint, not in a live region, so it is not read out on each keystroke.
- A refused question (400) is a field error under the textarea.
