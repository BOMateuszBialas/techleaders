# Saved recommendations

## What it is for

"Your recommendations": the owner's saved and completed recommendations, newest first, so the owner can come back to them after the answer is gone.

## Rules

- An empty list says where recommendations will appear. A list that could not be loaded is an `ErrorState` with what to do next, and it never shows a technical detail.
- Each row says when it was suggested, in the store's time zone, and carries its status in words.
- The owner decides: the actions only save, dismiss or complete a recommendation. Nothing in the store changes.
