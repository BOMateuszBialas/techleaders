# Recommendation card

## What it is for

One recommendation from Copilot, in the answer and in "Your recommendations". The owner reads what was observed, the evidence, the reasoning and the suggested action, then decides.

## Rules

- The priority is a word ("High priority") on a neutral badge. The status is a word ("Status: Saved") on its badge. Colour is never the only signal (D3, D4).
- The status badge shows the status the owner has in the page, so the answer and the saved list agree.
- Copilot suggests; the owner decides. The card changes nothing in the store: the actions only save, dismiss or complete the recommendation.
- Model text is plain text, never HTML.
