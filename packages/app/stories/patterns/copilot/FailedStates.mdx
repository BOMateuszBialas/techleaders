# Failed states

## What it is for

What the owner sees when the AI cannot answer: the provider failed, timed out, refused or returned an answer that failed our checks. The rule-based answer still works, so the owner is never left with nothing.

## Rules

- A failed AI call is an information state, not an error: a `warning` or `info` notice above the rule-based answer, never `ErrorState` and never `destructive`.
- The notice names the reason in the owner's words. It never shows a raw provider error, a status code or a token.
- The rule-based summary and its figures stay on the page under the notice.
